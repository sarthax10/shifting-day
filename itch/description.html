Everything you own.
One small truck.

You're moving out of a falling-down house and into a manor by the sea. Everything you own goes on the back of one small truck, along with the cat, and the road between here and there has hills, toll gates, level crossings and cliffs. Every bump shifts the load.

Stack it well, tie it down, drive it gently, and catch whatever bounces off before it's gone for good.

Pack

Drag things from the pile onto the truck. Turn them to fit, stack them, and tie the wobbly ones down with rope. Fragile things break, and pets have opinions.

Drive

A real gearbox. Let the handbrake off, pull away in first and change up when the gear light goes red. Stop at toll gates and level crossings with the handbrake on. Stall it in too high a gear and you start again in first.

Climb & catch

The roof-mounted anchor crossbow fires a grapple. Shoot the top of a cliff that's too steep to drive, then reel the truck up it. Shoot something that's just fallen off and it's reeled straight back onto the bed.

Deliver

Park between the cones at each stop and the leg is scored. Whatever fell off waits in the stop's Lost & Found, and you buy it back with the bubble wrap you pick up on the road. Five legs, five scores, one total, and then your name on the leaderboard.

Scoring

Every stop banks a score for the leg you just drove. The trip's score is the five legs added up.

On each legPoints
Everything on the truck and in one pieceits worth, again at every stop
Parking square between the cones+10 to +100
Clean leg: nothing fell off or broke+50
Speed bonus: every second at 50 km/h or more+5 a second
Anything that broke on this legminus half its worth

Whatever you carry safely pays at every stop, and something lost early costs you every leg after, unless you buy it back. The speed bonus takes top gear, and the faster you take the bumps, the more shakes loose.

Biscuit the cat is worth the most (250), then the grandfather clock (220), Sunny the canary (200), Goldie the fish (160) and the fridge (150). The boxes are worth 20 to 40. 1,500 points for one star, 3,000 for two, 4,500 for three.

A run clock counts the whole move, packing included. Time is kept apart from points: emptying the truck to race never tops the score board.

The leaderboard

At the end of the trip, press LEADERBOARD, put your name on the run and send it. The boards are also on the title menu, any time.

Tap a name to see the run: every leg's time and score, how it was made, what fell off or broke where, and which pets made it home. Every run is checked on the way in, so the numbers on the board are numbers the game can really make.
