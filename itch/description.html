Everything you own.
One small truck.

You're moving out of a falling-down house and into a manor by the sea. Everything you own goes on the back of one small truck, along with the cat, and the road between here and there has hills, toll gates, level crossings and cliffs. Every bump shifts the load.

Stack it well, tie it down, drive it gently, and catch whatever bounces off before it's gone for good.

Pack

Drag things from the pile onto the truck. Turn them to fit, stack them, and tie the wobbly ones down with rope. Fragile things break, and pets have opinions.

Drive

A real gearbox. Let the handbrake off, pull away in first and change up when the gear light goes red. Stop at toll gates and level crossings with the handbrake on. Stall it in too high a gear and you start again in first.

Climb & catch

The roof-mounted anchor crossbow fires a grapple. Shoot the top of a cliff that's too steep to drive, then reel the truck up it. Shoot something that's just fallen off and it's reeled straight back onto the bed.

Deliver

Park between the cones at each stop. Whatever fell off waits in the stop's Lost & Found, and you buy it back with the bubble wrap you pick up on the road. Beat the clock, beat your best run, and get home in one piece.
