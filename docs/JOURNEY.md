# Shifting Day: the journey

The jam game becomes one trip in five legs. Each leg is a different biome, at a different time of day,
with different weather. Every leg except the last ends at a roadside stop where you park with the
handbrake on, and the trip is saved there. Your cargo carries over from leg to leg.

| Leg | Biome | Time and weather | Driving focus | Checkpoint |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Old Street (tutorial) | Meadow farmland, windmills | Sunny afternoon | Learn gears, handbrake, toll gates | Corner Fuel |
| 2 Maple Valley | Autumn forest, waterfall, stone bridge, river | Misty morning, light shafts, falling leaves | Rolling climbs, a long descent | Maple Mill Fuel & Pie |
| 3 Frostpeak Pass | Snowy peaks, snow-capped pines, frozen lake, cabin | Blue hour, falling snow, aurora | Icy road: brakes are weaker, steep hill starts | Summit Lodge |
| 4 Neon Town | Skyline, rooftops, neon shops, cinema | Rainy night, puddles, lightning | Stop-and-go at rail crossings, speed bumps, wet brakes | Starlite Diner |
| 5 Coast Road | Turquoise sea, white cliffs, lighthouse, dunes, beach huts | Bright morning, gulls, sea glitter | Rolling cliffs, a final climb, park at the new house | The new house |

The trip takes two days. You leave in the afternoon, cross the valley in the morning mist, top the pass
at dusk under the aurora, drive through town in the night rain, and reach the sea in the morning.

## What the references taught (Pinterest, November 2026)

- **Mountains (Firewatch style):** layers defined by value alone, each further layer closer to the sky's
  colour, with mist pooled between them. A big soft sun, and dark tree silhouettes framing the foreground.
- **Autumn:** waterfalls are the hero, with stepped cascades and mist at the base. Saturated orange and
  red canopies against cool blue-green water, and wooden or stone bridges.
- **Snow and aurora:** the aurora is a curtain from green into violet over dark-blue peaks. Frozen lakes
  mirror the sky, a lone cabin has a warm window, and snow-capped pines stand out.
- **Rainy town:** wet streets double every light, with long vertical reflections. Each storefront has its
  own neon colour, and warm windows sit against blue rain.
- **Coast:** a turquoise-to-deep-blue sea with a glitter path, a striped lighthouse on a green-topped
  cliff, and white surf against rocks.
- **Effects:** snow and rain in two layers (near and far), drifting fog bands, light shafts, twinkling
  lights. Every biome should have something moving at every depth.

## Rules for every biome

1. Five or more depth layers, each lighter and closer to the sky colour the further back it sits.
2. One hero light (sun, moon, aurora, neon) that everything else answers to.
3. Something animated at every depth: far (clouds, aurora, boats), middle (waterfall, birds, smoke),
   near (leaves, snow, rain, gulls).
4. A unique foreground strip and roadside props, so the road edge itself tells you where you are.
5. The road and soil take the biome's colours: leaf-strewn, snow-packed, wet asphalt, sandy.
